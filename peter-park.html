PETER PARK

Peter Park: California Bar Exam at Age 17

Peter Park became the youngest person in California history to pass the California Bar Exam at the time of his achievement.

A remarkable path

Peter began law school at age 13 while continuing his education. Public reporting describes how he used an accelerated educational path, including CLEP and the California High School Proficiency Exam, before completing law school and preparing for the Bar Exam.

He took the California Bar Exam in July 2023 and learned in November that he had passed. He was 17 when he passed and later became a prosecutor in the Tulare County District Attorney's Office.

What the reporting says

The Washington Post reported that Peter started law school at 13 and passed the California Bar Exam at 17. The Los Angeles Times likewise reported that he passed on his first attempt and described his accelerated educational route.
