What is this book about?

Fast-Track Attorney: Passing the Bar at Age 17 tells the story behind an accelerated educational path that led Peter Park and his siblings toward legal education and the California Bar Exam years earlier than the traditional college-to-law-school route.

Peter Park passed the California Bar Exam at age 17, becoming the youngest person to pass the exam at the time. His younger sister Sophia Park then surpassed his record, passing the California Bar Exam at 17 years and 8 months.

The story did not end with the Bar Exam. Peter and Sophia went on to become practicing attorneys and prosecutors. Their unusual educational journey—from early law study and California's alternative pathway into legal education to bar preparation and professional practice—is the foundation of this book.

From youngest bar passers to prosecutors

Peter Park passed the California Bar Exam at 17 and went on to practice as a Deputy District Attorney. He is now serving as a prosecutor with the Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office.

Sophia Park broke Peter's record by passing the California Bar Exam at 17 years and 8 months. After becoming a licensed attorney, she also went on to serve as a prosecutor with the Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office.

This later chapter is important to understanding the story: the goal was not simply to set a record on the California Bar Exam, but to build a path from accelerated education into the actual practice of law and public service.

An Essential Guide: Top-Down Parenting: Raising California’s Youngest Attorneys

An Essential Guide: Top-Down Parenting: Raising California’s Youngest Attorneys explains the educational framework and parenting philosophy behind a remarkable outcome: children passing the California Bar Exam at 17 and beginning careers as attorneys and prosecutors at an unusually young age.

At the heart of the book is a change in the parent's perspective. Rather than allowing a child's past performance to define what seems possible, the Top-Down method begins with a vision of the destination and works backward to determine what education, habits, skills, and experiences are needed to reach it. The emphasis is not simply on working harder, but on learning to live in the direction of the future you want to create.

The Root: The Mindset

The first part of the book focuses on the ideas that make the practical methods possible. It explores the power of teleology—the idea that a child's future can be shaped by purpose and direction rather than being limited by past mistakes or conventional expectations.

The book also introduces a distinctive way of thinking about time and vision: casting the “flower petals” of a desired future forward so that the vision gradually becomes part of the decisions made in the present. The destination becomes a reference point for today's choices.

The Branches: Practical Methods

The second part turns that mindset into practical parenting and educational strategies. The 80/20 Efficiency Rule focuses on obtaining strong results without consuming all of a child's time and energy. By concentrating effort on the activities that matter most, children can make rapid progress while still leaving room for music, entrepreneurship, creativity, relationships, and other interests.

The book also presents the “One Question” Mission: cultivating a lifelong habit of asking meaningful questions, pursuing the answers, and developing the curiosity required for genuine mastery. The objective is not merely to complete assignments or accumulate credentials, but to develop children who can continue learning independently.

“Thought becomes reality.” The Top-Down approach is presented as a blueprint not only for raising future attorneys, but for helping children pursue whatever meaningful destination they choose—whether that is law, entrepreneurship, music, science, or another creative and professional path.

Ultimately, the goal is broader than academic achievement. The book describes a vision of children becoming capable, happy, grateful, and committed to contributing to society.
