What is this book about?

Fast-Track Attorney: Passing the Bar at Age 17 tells the story behind an accelerated educational path that led Peter Park and his siblings toward legal education and the California Bar Exam years earlier than the traditional college-to-law-school route.

Peter Park passed the California Bar Exam at age 17, becoming the youngest person to pass the exam at the time. His younger sister Sophia Park then surpassed his record, passing the California Bar Exam at 17 years and 8 months.

The story did not end with the Bar Exam. Peter and Sophia went on to become practicing attorneys and prosecutors. Their unusual educational journey—from early law study and California's alternative pathway into legal education to bar preparation and professional practice—is the foundation of this book.

From youngest bar passers to prosecutors

Peter Park passed the California Bar Exam at 17 and went on to practice as a Deputy District Attorney. He is now serving as a prosecutor with the Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office.

Sophia Park broke Peter's record by passing the California Bar Exam at 17 years and 8 months. After becoming a licensed attorney, she also went on to serve as a prosecutor with the Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office.

This later chapter is important to understanding the story: the goal was not simply to set a record on the California Bar Exam, but to build a path from accelerated education into the actual practice of law and public service.

An Essential Guide: Top-Down Parenting: Raising California’s Youngest Attorneys

An Essential Guide: Top-Down Parenting explores the parenting philosophy, educational strategy, and practical decisions behind raising children who pursued an accelerated path toward becoming California attorneys.

Together, the two books provide both the original story and a broader guide to the educational approach behind the family's journey.
