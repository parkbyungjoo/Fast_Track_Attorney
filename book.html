What is this book about?

This book tells the story of how Dr. Byungjoo Park's children pursued an accelerated path toward legal education and the California Bar Exam without following the usual college-to-law-school route.

Published descriptions say the book provides a step-by-step account of the family's approach, including the educational requirements and preparation involved in the journey.
