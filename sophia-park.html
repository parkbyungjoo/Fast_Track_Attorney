SOPHIA PARK

Sophia Park: California Bar Exam at 17 Years and 8 Months

Sophia Park passed the California Bar Exam at 17 years and 8 months, a few months younger than her brother Peter when he passed. She is now a Deputy District Attorney with the Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office.

Following the same path

According to ABC News and the Tulare County District Attorney's Office, Sophia enrolled in law school at age 13 while attending high school. She later completed high school and law school and passed the July 2024 California Bar Exam.

Her achievement followed Peter's 2023 result and attracted national and California media coverage. At the time, reports described Sophia as the youngest person to pass the California General Bar Exam. She was subsequently sworn in as an attorney and began her prosecutorial career in Tulare County before moving to the Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office.

From law student to prosecutor

Public reporting said Sophia planned to follow her brother's path toward becoming a prosecutor. She subsequently worked as a Deputy District Attorney in Tulare County and is now listed as a Deputy District Attorney with the Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office.
